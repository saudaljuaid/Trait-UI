Saud Aljuaid's own drawing of the OpenRFS fish. It is copied here from
the website repository, where it is produced from the original
assets/img/logo-source.jpeg (1600 x 1049, the drawing itself) by that
repository's tools/make-mark.py, which trims the image to its ink and
lifts the white ground to transparency. Nothing is recoloured,
outlined, rotated or composited.

tools/make-logo.py reduces this file to the characters gfetch prints,
and src/trait_logo.h is the result. Four things in the drawing become
four characters - the paper, the black outline, the red body and the
pink tongue - because a luminance ramp turns a flat red fish into a
featureless blob.

logo the project was given. It is unused since the panel it was the
menu button for was deleted, and is kept because it is original work.
