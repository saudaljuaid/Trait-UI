openrfs-logo-source.jpeg is the OpenRFS mark: Saud Aljuaid's own
drawing of the fish, 1600 x 1049, sha256

  0b0338127f35c5655376a9d18b16e9398558032679fd8b5ff7c6bf79d88302b3

copied untouched from the website repository, where the same file is
the source of every mark-*.png and the favicon.

openrfs-mark-512.png is that repository's 512-pixel build of it, kept
here for reference. gfetch does NOT use it, and the reason is worth
writing down: the build lifts the drawing's white to transparency, and
the fish's eyes are white, so in the trimmed mark the eyes are holes.
A reduction of it comes out with a blank-faced fish. That is invisible
on a white page and it is not invisible anywhere else.

tools/make-logo.py therefore reads the drawing. Five things in it
become five characters - the paper, the black outline, the red body,
the pink tongue and the white of an eye - because a luminance ramp
turns a flat red fish into a featureless blob. Which white is paper and
which is an eye is answered by a flood fill from the border: paper is
the white you can reach from the edge. src/trait_logo.h is the result,
and the colours in it are the mean of every pixel that fell into each
class rather than values anybody picked.
