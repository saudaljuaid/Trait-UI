onion.png (the mark) and ../wallpaper/wallpaper.png are the project
owner's own artwork, drawn by them, and committed by them directly.

They arrived under the names onion_vegetables_16828.png and
debian_onion_color_v2.png and were moved into this tree's layout; the
files themselves are unchanged, byte for byte.

The mark is a PICTURE rather than a silhouette - it carries its own
colours - which matters to anything drawing it: tinting it to a single
ink, the way a flat mark is drawn, turns it into a blob.  See
src/kernel/trait_panel.c in the Phipia OS tree, where that is exactly
what happened the first time it went through that path.
